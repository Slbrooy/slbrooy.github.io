Rhizome Springs is an off-grid community
on Salt Spring Island, British Columbia.

The land is being nurtured as a space for people to gather, explore, build, learn, live and grow into new regenerative ways of being through participatory workshops, land development and creative events.

What does it mean to live in harmony?

In harmony with the land that feeds us, the air that breathes us, the water that enlivens us...

At Rhizome Springs, the land is our laboratory, the community our teachers and our bodies the compass. We are dedicated to the ongoing inquiry into how we relate to ourselves, each other and the land we live on. Through the lenses of ecology, creativity and embodiment, we continuously explore what it means to be in reciprocity with the living world around us.

We've just begun planting this seed, would you like to help us tend to it?

Rhizome Springs is in early development stages. We are building infrastructure, growing food systems, and creating gathering spaces. If you feel called to be part of this living project, we would love to hear from you.
