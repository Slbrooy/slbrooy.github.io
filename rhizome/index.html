Welcome to Rhizome Springs

Rhizome Springs is an off-grid community and gathering place on Salt Spring Island, BC. We host workshops, dance festivals, ecological learning, creative residencies, and community gatherings rooted in our connection to the land.

Our Pillars

Embodiment

Dance, movement, and somatic practices that deepen our relationship with our bodies and each other.

Ecology

Learning to live in reciprocity with the land through permaculture, restoration, and ecological stewardship.

Creativity

Art, music, building, and making as pathways to deeper understanding and community connection.
