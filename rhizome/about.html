About Us

Our Vision

To seed a future where we live in integrity with ourselves, each other, and nature – and in appreciation of their intricate connections.

Our Mission

To cultivate a living process of inquiry exploring resilient and regenerative forms of community, through the lenses of ecology, creativity and embodiment.

The Land

Rhizome Springs is situated on the south side of Mt. Tuam on Salt Spring Island, within the unceded territories of the Coast Salish peoples. The 20-acre property features old-growth forest, natural springs, meadows, and a growing network of gardens and gathering spaces.

We live off-grid, powered by solar energy and spring water. Our buildings are hand-built using natural materials. We steward this land with care, guided by principles of ecological restoration and permaculture.

Our Ethos

We believe in living, working and playing mindfully with ourselves, each other and the land

We are committed to cultivating an inclusive, self-sufficient and regenerative community. We value diversity of thought, practice and background. We seek to learn from the land and from each other, honouring both ancient wisdom and creative innovation.

Embodiment

Soma: the body experienced from within, at the level of cellular awareness. Through practices of meditation, Contact Improvisation, Body-Mind Centering, dance, yoga, and somatic exploration, we deepen our relationship with our bodies and each other.

Community

Rhizome Springs is a co-creation. Every structure has been built by volunteers, every garden tended by community members. We welcome people who want to contribute their skills, energy, and curiosity to this ongoing experiment in living differently.

Our Stewards

Selena La Brooy

Pattern interrupter and realignment guide

Selena is the founder and steward of Rhizome Springs. Her path has woven through dance, healing arts, herbalism, and ecological building. A certified Rolfer and lifelong mover, Selena's vision for Rhizome Springs grew from a deep passion for community, embodied living, and tending the land.
