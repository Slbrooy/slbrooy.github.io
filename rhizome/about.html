About Rhizome Springs

The Land

Rhizome Springs sits on 20 acres of forested land on Salt Spring Island, in the traditional territory of the Hul'q'umi'num' and SENCOŤEN speaking peoples. The property features old-growth forest, natural springs, meadows, and a growing network of gardens and gathering spaces.

We steward this land with care, guided by principles of ecological restoration and permaculture. Our buildings are hand-built using natural materials, and we live off-grid, powered by solar energy and spring water.

Our Vision

Rhizome Springs is a living laboratory for regenerative culture. We believe that by deepening our relationship with the land, our bodies, and each other, we can discover new ways of living that are both sustaining and joyful. Through workshops, festivals, residencies, and daily practice, we explore what it means to be fully alive in community.

What We Do

Embodiment

Movement, Dance & Somatic Practice

We host contact improvisation jams, fusion dance festivals, and somatic workshops throughout the year. Our outdoor dance platform and indoor studio provide spaces for movers of all levels to explore their embodied experience. From ecstatic dance to structured technique classes, our programming invites participants to listen deeply to their bodies and discover new ways of moving together.

Ecology

Land Stewardship & Permaculture

Our ecological programming includes permaculture design courses, forest restoration work parties, wild food foraging walks, and natural building workshops. We practice and teach regenerative land management, seed saving, and food preservation. Living off-grid means learning to work with natural systems rather than against them.

Creativity

Art, Music & Making

Creativity is woven into everything we do at Rhizome Springs. From music jams around the fire to collaborative art projects, from woodworking to weaving, we believe that making things together builds community and deepens our understanding of the world. Our residency program invites artists and makers to spend time on the land, creating work inspired by this place.

Selena La Brooy

Selena is the founder and steward of Rhizome Springs. A certified Rolfer, dancer, and ecological builder, she has been tending this land since 2018. Her vision for Rhizome Springs grew from a lifelong passion for movement, community, and living close to the earth.

When not hosting events or working on the land, Selena maintains a Rolfing practice on Salt Spring Island, helping people find ease and alignment in their bodies.
