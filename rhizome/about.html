About Us

Our Vision

To seed a future where we live in integrity with ourselves, each other, and nature – and in appreciation of their intricate connections.

Our Mission

To cultivate a living process of inquiry exploring resilient and regenerative forms of embodiment, creativity and ecology – to discover what it means to live in a more harmonious and connected way of being.

The Land

Nestled deep in the forest atop Mt. Tuam, on Salt Spring Island, we make our home in the unceded and traditional territories of the Coast Salish People (Hul'qumi'num and SENCOFEN speaking people). Surrounded by nature reserves and a Buddhist Retreat Centre, we enjoy magical vistas and daily sunsets over Vancouver Island and the Coast Salish Sea.

We operate as a rustic off-grid community and strive to live, work and play in harmony and respect with nature and the mindful consumption of elements (water, wood, power, etc).

Our Ethos

We believe in living, working and playing mindfully with ourselves, each other, and the natural elements around us.

We strive to consider how our actions and choices affect others and strive to listen to those with quieter voices. We strive for self-sufficiency and regeneration as we make our decisions regarding our power and water use, waste management, building materials, and food systems. We believe in inclusivity, sobriety, compassion, self responsibility and honesty in all our relating.

Ecology

The study of relationships between organisms and their environments.

Learn more
  • Understanding optimal and viable regenerative potential
  • Growing food forests and employing permaculture principals for nourishment, medicinal support and symbiotic relationships
  • Working with natural resources for building, power generation and water management
  • Learning from first nations about traditional ways of life and practices
  • Hub for workshops and learning opportunities related to permaculture and regenerative agriculture
  • Trading station for food, medicines with like-minded communities

Community

We are co-creating the magic we envision by coming together. This project grows through the wonderful people who offer their time and expertise to help craft the landscape, plant gardens and make magic happen.

Our Stewards

Selena La Brooy

Pattern interrupter and realignment guide

Selena is fascinated by patterns in nature and behaviour. Believing in the inherent desire for peace and harmony in all beings and perpetually curious about everything, she is passionate about creating a container for on-going inquiry into how to live in a more symbiotic and regenerative way with all of life. She has a deep love of Contact Improvisation, performance and holistic healing. She is a dancer, a healer, a teacher, an entrepreneur, a herbalist, a gardener and a clown.
